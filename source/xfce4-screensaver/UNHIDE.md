# Screensaver desktop file

Leave `Hidden=` off of `vaultos-arch-spin.desktop` if you want the theme to show up.

Match the stock floaters/slideshow desktop shape, with Exec/TryExec pointed at `/usr/lib/xfce4-screensaver/vaultos-arch-spin`.

Do not drop a local override of `xfce-floaters.desktop` under `~/.local/share/applications/screensavers/`.
