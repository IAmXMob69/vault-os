# Terminal accessibility

1. Default text is readable: phosphor `#1AFF6B` on vault-black `#070807`.
2. Bloom, scanlines, and persistence are never required to read the terminal.
3. Focus indication must not rely on glow alone (panel/GTK handle chrome focus).

Use `vaultos-terminal-phosphor reduced` or `clear` when CRT effects should stay off. Desktop and lock spin are controlled separately and must not be forced off by the terminal dial.
